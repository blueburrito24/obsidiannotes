- [ ] >[!tacos]-
	>- old el paso soft taco tortillas 1.85gbp for 8pcs
	>- shazan mince beef 500g 5.24
	>- galbani italian mozzarella 125g 1.94
	>- island sun farina potato starch 500g 60p
	>- asda iceberg lettuce
	(total; 10.52gbp)
	following [this video](https://www.instagram.com/p/DE-kWr5yOl-/)
	chop up two onions and put them in an oiled pan, 
	pour in the mince beef and break it up with a wooden spatula, letting it cook til brown, 
	then drop in all the spices and mix alongside some starch, pour in some water to deglaze the pan and spread the spices in the beef, 
	put a tortilla in another oiled pan and fry til brown and puffy, 
	then on the other side put on some mozzarella and the beef on top, fold the tortilla while it's packed and fry until brown, then spread some chopped lettuce on top
	^ based on this recipe, using all the beef in the package would be good to make up to six or seven tacos, so these ingredients can last two or even three weeks as I would prefer to only make four tacos per week - for two tacos per day, and I would only eat tacos on two days of the week.
	
	>[!potato chips]-
	>- asda baby potatoes 1kg 99p
	following [this video](https://www.instagram.com/joey_wellness/reel/DcLyiBwxyeg/)
	i need to boil the baby potatoes in a pot for 25 minutes (until fork tender), 
	preheat the oven to 200 degrees, 
	drain the baby potatoes and lay them onto parchment paper on the oven tray, 
	smash the baby potatoes with a glass (to the point they are still intact but spread apart), 
	pour olive oil on top alongside seasoning, 
	then bake for 40 minutes (until golden brown) and top it with some shredded cheese
	^ this can last me three weeks possibly, as I would only eat maybe six pcs per week - three pcs per day for two days to accompany other meals (not the tacos)
	
	>[!smoothie yoghurt thing]-

	>- asda raspberries 300g 2.96gbp
	>- asda chia seeds 150g 1.54gbp
	>- asda just essentials porridge oats 1kg 85p

	drop in like five pcs of strawberries, ten pcs of tropical smoothie mix, twelve pcs of raspberries, half a teaspoon of chia seeds, a quarter cup of porridge oats, some ice cubes and a little bit of milk - blend til smoothie, spread some white chocolate chips on top and enjoy
	
	>[!chicago deep dish pizza]-
	>- asda just essentials plain flour 1.5kg 70p
	>- natco fine corn meal 1kg 1.98gbp
	>- allinson fast action yeast 100g 1.50gbp
	>- asda grated mozzarella 250g 2.45gbp
	>- asda just essentials hard cheese 170g 2.42gbp
	>- mutti finely chopped tomatoes 400g 1.55gbp
	>- masterclass 23cm deep pie dish 9in 12.99gbp
	>- rellapart pizza stone 33x33cm 18.99gbp
	(total; 42.58gbp including the deep dish pan and pizza stone, 10.60gbp without them)
	following this [this video](https://www.instagram.com/rachaellsrecipes/reel/C353i98vk9b/) alongside [this video](https://www.instagram.com/rafifronz/reel/DZVX2fEq9y1/)
	mix three and a half caps of plain flour with half a cup of fine corn meal, two teaspoons of salt and two tablespoons of melted unsalted butter, a quarter cup of warm water, two quarter teaspoons of fast action yeast, two teaspoons of sugar and one tablespoon of olive oil, in a big metal bowl til it's a real dough
	knead the dough, on a regular surface or a chopping board, until it's smooth and can be pulled,
	leave the dough in the same metal bowl, with olive oil spread beforehand in the bowl, and let it rest for thirty minutes,
	spread some plain flour on the surface or chopping board and lay the dough on there, split it in half with a knife so we can use the dough later,
	get a rolling pin and spread out one half of the dough,
	lay this flat dough on the deep dish pan and fold the rim of the dough that falls off the deep dish pan on itself to create a crust,
	then spread a bunch of the mozzarella cheese inside the pan and lay the second flattened dough on top,
	take a knife and cut in three air slits in the second layer of dough, and cut off the dough laying off on the outer rim of the deep dish,
	spread the canned tomatoes all over the pan on the second layer of dough,
	put the deep dish pan on the pizza stone and leave to cook in the oven til the crust is visibly brown, then take it out and grate some hard cheese over the pizza
	^ I can eat some slices of it at uni and eat some at home, one pizza can last me a week
	
	>[!brownies]-
	>- asda bake cocoa powder 250g 3.48gbp
	>- asda bake dark chocolate chips 100g 1.32gbp
	(total; 4.80gbp, other ingredients are typical household items)
	following [this video](https://www.instagram.com/the_plant_slant/reel/DcPj3s0RYif/)
	drop four eggs into a bowl, whisk with two cups of sugar (420g), one cup of melted salted butter (22g), two thirds of a cup of cocoa powder (62g) and three quarters of a cup of all purpose flour (98g),
	preheat oven to 175 degrees and lay some parchment paper on a pan,
	pour in your brownie mix and throw on some dark chocolate chips on top, bake for 40 minutes until fudgy in the centre, let it cool and enjoy
	^ this is a ton of brownies, enough to have over a week or two
